§ 03 — Acquisition

We buy the cars we'd sell.

Direct purchase. Cleared funds in 48 hours. Brokerage available when patience favors the seller.

A straightforward buyout offer from a house that has been writing checks for forty-eight years — with the option to move to brokerage should the market favour patience.

  1. i.

    Enclosed Transport, Nationwide

    We collect from driveways in Miami, Manhattan, Seattle, Austin, Milwaukee — wherever the car is. Eight in ten never see an open trailer.

  2. ii.

    Offer Within 24 Hours

    Reviewed by a principal buyer, not a call-center. Documented, in writing. Cleared funds in 48 hours.

  3. iii.

    Brokerage, When Wise

    If buyout doesn't clear your number, we place the car on consignment under house terms — retaining the option, never defaulting to it.
